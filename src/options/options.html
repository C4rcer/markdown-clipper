Right-click menu and shortcut

Right-click a page or a selection and choose "Clip ... as Markdown", or press not set. With text selected, the shortcut clips the selection; otherwise the article.

To change the shortcut, open Firefox's Add-ons and themes page (Ctrl+Shift+A), click the gear at the top and choose "Manage Extension Shortcuts".

Privacy

Excerpt reads a page only when you click its button, its menu entry or its shortcut there. The conversion happens inside Firefox on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere: no ads, no analytics, no account. Images stay where they are; the Markdown only links to them.

Support

Excerpt is free, has no ads and collects no data. If it saves you time, a tip on Ko-fi keeps it maintained. A rating on Firefox Add-ons also helps other people find it.
