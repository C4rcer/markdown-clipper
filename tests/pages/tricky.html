Tom's "Tide": notes #1

These are working notes on the first chapter, written on the ferry and tidied up at home, so some of the times are approximate and some of the names are surely spelled wrong.

The chapter opens at slack water. Nothing moves, the gulls sit on the mud, and the narrator counts the minutes until the harbor fills again, which takes him most of a page and all of his patience.

What follows is a list of everything he gets wrong about the moon, in the order he gets it wrong, with the page numbers from the 1994 paperback edition for anyone who wants to check.
